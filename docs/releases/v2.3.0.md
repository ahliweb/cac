# Release v2.3.0 — 2026-11-19

SMA coding syllabus v1.2 with tracks C, D and E, and two companion modules
that close the CP gap F-LD-d and F-LD-e. This is a **minor** release: **no
breaking changes**, and **no OSN curriculum content changed**.

This document only covers what changed between v2.2.0 and v2.3.0; read
[`docs/releases/v2.2.0.md`](v2.2.0.md) for the previous release.

## Upgrade notes

None required. Run `bun install` after pulling the tag. Things that behave
differently:

- `osn validate --program sma-coding` checks the v1.2 corpus: five tracks
  (A–E), 364 track weeks, 26 CP mappings and 9 enrichment links. Corpus
  files written for v1.1 no longer validate (`tracks.json` requires
  `startClass` and `cpStatus`; `cp-mapping.json` uses `partialTracks`
  instead of `partial`).
- `osn plan --program sma-coding` and `osn render lembar-siswa --program
  sma-coding` accept tracks C, D and E. `--track E` with `--kelas X` or a
  grade-X semester exits 2, because track E runs in grades XI–XII only.

## What is new

### Owner decisions (2026-11-19)

- **Tracks C, D and E approved** with every author recommendation (#123;
  #68, #84, #88) and merged into
  [`docs/programs/sma-coding/silabus.md`](../programs/sma-coding/silabus.md)
  v1.2.0. The decisions per open question are in its bagian 9.3; the draft
  documents stay as history.
  - Track C, IoT and robotics: ESP32 DevKit (Pico as fallback), C++ in
    Arduino style, mixed procurement between partner and school, separate
    lab network.
  - Track D, AI: Orange and unplugged activities in grade X, Python in
    XI–XII. Online AI services are teacher demonstrations only, with no
    student accounts. No faces or voices of students as training data.
  - Track E, quantum computing: grades XI–XII only, week-code prefix `E-`.
    Quirk plus a simulator written in C++ or TypeScript. Qiskit and cloud
    quantum are teacher demonstrations through a school account only.
  - Tracks D and E are **enrichment outside the CP wording**. The data marks
    them `cpStatus: "pengayaan"` and records their overlaps as
    `enrichmentLinks`. The audit rejects them in CP mappings, so no CP item
    is claimed as covered by them.
- **CP gap F-LD-d and F-LD-e for track A** closed by cross-track companion
  modules (#122, #105).

### Data, schema and CLI

- `tracks.json`: `startClass` (`X` or `XI`) and `cpStatus` (`dalam-cp` or
  `pengayaan`).
- `cp-mapping.json`: per-track `partialTracks` and `enrichmentLinks`.
- `osn-bridge.json`: `trackBridges` for track C.
- `bun run check:sma-fidelity` parses the new tables. A synthetic one-cell
  drift in each new track fails as expected.
- `plan` and `render lembar-siswa` print an enrichment note for tracks D and
  E.

### Modules

Two draft modules, `pendamping-merancang-kebutuhan-sistem` (F-LD-d) and
`pendamping-keamanan-lanjut-konektivitas` (F-LD-e), bring the draft
`sma-coding` modules to 29.
